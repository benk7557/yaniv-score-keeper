Privacy Policy

Yaniv Score Keeper · Last updated: 9 November 2026

Yaniv Score Keeper ("the app") is a score-keeping tool for the card game Yaniv. This policy explains what information is handled when you use it. In short: the app itself does not collect your personal data, and your game data never leaves your device. The app shows ads through Google AdMob, and AdMob may process some device information to serve them (details below).

Your game data stays on your device

Player names, scores, game settings (score limit, language, display mode) and your win history are saved only in the app's local storage on your phone so they are there next time you open it. We do not receive this data and cannot see it. The app has no accounts or sign-in.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows a banner advertisement on the game screen, provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction and diagnostic data. This is handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy; we do not receive or store it, and we never send your names or scores to Google or anyone else.

  • Consent. Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom) the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before requesting ads. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Ads & Privacy inside the app when it is shown.
  • Personalised ads. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in Android Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts, no sign-in.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting services of our own.
  • No access to your contacts, precise location, camera, microphone, photos or files.
  • No sale of personal data. The game works fully offline; ads simply do not appear without a connection.

Permissions the app uses

  • Internet / network state — only so the advertising SDK can load ads.
  • Advertising ID — used by the advertising SDK as described above.
  • Vibration — for tap feedback.
  • Keep screen awake — so the display does not turn off mid-game.
  • Motion sensor (optional "Wake on Pickup" setting) — readings are used on-device only to dismiss the screensaver and are never stored or sent anywhere.

Deleting your data

Use Clear History inside the app to erase win history, or clear the app's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the app. Because your game data is stored nowhere else, that removes everything we could hold. For data held by Google, use your Google Account's ad settings.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.
